This site is a running, always-evolving exhibition documenting the Trump presidencies:
the statements, the moments, the decisions, and the general vibe of living through what
this site's author considers the dumbest chapter in American political history.

Here's what you'll find as this place grows:

- **Writings** (you're reading one) — essays and running commentary on the current state of things.
- **Gallery** — photos of Donald Trump looking, saying, or doing something dumb.
- **The Roast** — video clips with running commentary, MST3K-style.
- **Polls** — vote on the dumbest moments, and settle arguments about who's dumber than who.

More to come. This is a living document.
