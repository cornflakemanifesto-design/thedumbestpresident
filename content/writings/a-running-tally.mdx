It's easy for the sheer volume of dumb things to blur together into background noise.
The point of writing it down — dating it, filing it, keeping a tally — is to resist that
blur. Future readers (including future you) deserve a record that isn't just vibes.

Some ground rules this site tries to follow, and that you may want to keep in mind
as you write your own posts:

1. **Cite specifics.** Dates, sources, direct quotes. It's funnier and it's defensible.
2. **Mock the statement, not people who had nothing to do with it.**
3. **Opinion is clearly opinion.** This is a satire and commentary site, not a wire service.
